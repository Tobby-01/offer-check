02 — How it decides

Four passes. One verdict.

An AI never makes the call. Fixed rules score the evidence, so the same page always gets the same answer. Plain-language advice sits on top and can't change the verdict.

  2. 02

  3. 03

  4. 04

    Proof of what it saw

    Evidence you can check

    Every report carries a SHA-256 hash of the exact page snapshot and a commitment over the URL, verdict, score, findings and time. If anyone questions the call, the evidence is on record.

04 — FAQ

Straight answers.

Does "Looks OK" mean it's safe?

No. It means no red flags were found. Some drainers hide from scanners, so still never share a seed phrase, never pay a fee to unlock earnings, and use a fresh burner wallet for new mints.

Do I need to connect my wallet?

Never. Offer Check only reads the public page. It doesn't connect, sign or submit anything, and it never asks for your details.

Why would an honest site get flagged?

Brand-new domains and cheap TLDs add a little risk on their own. Each finding shows its weight and the exact text it matched, so you can judge for yourself. Anti-scam warnings on honest sites are recognised and ignored.
